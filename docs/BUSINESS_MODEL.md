# Business model

The farmer pays a one-time ₹500 registration fee. A successful payment activates membership without manual approval. The fee is stored as 50,000 paise.

When an eligible farmer or employee referral exists, 10% of the registration fee is credited as referral earnings. The initial credit is ₹50. Super admin can change the rate for future registrations; historical earnings retain their snapshotted rate and amount.

Employee-assisted cash flow: the employee receives ₹500 cash from the farmer, keeps that cash, and pays the company ₹500 online from a personal account. This is one fee and creates no later cash liability to the company.

Referral rewards are paid offline. The application records confirmed disbursements and calculates outstanding balances; it does not send payouts or accept online withdrawal claims.
