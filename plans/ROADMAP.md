# Delivery roadmap

## Phase 01 — Foundation and paid onboarding

- Brand-led public site and retained registration fields
- Fresh Supabase schema, encrypted Aadhaar storage and uniqueness controls
- Razorpay order, signature verification, captured-payment check and webhook recovery
- Immediate farmer/member activation and device-generated receipt
- Mobile/password login, referral earnings and employee onboarding dashboards

## Phase 02 — Operations console

- Staff account administration
- Manager farmer record correction with audited temporary employee grants
- Offline referral payout recording and allocation
- Commission-rate version management
- Cash collection reconciliation and exception queues

## Phase 03 — Production hardening

- Production Razorpay credentials and webhook configuration
- Seed first super admin through a controlled one-time runbook
- Automated browser, payment retry and role-isolation tests
- Monitoring, backups, incident and access-review runbooks

## Future, not enabled

- Farmer referral campaigns beyond the default programme
- Customer self-service dashboard beyond referral and payout visibility
- Online referral disbursement
