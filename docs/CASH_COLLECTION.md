# Cash collection

Cash collection is an operational acknowledgment, separate from payment status.

- The employee records receipt of exactly the registration fee from the farmer.
- The employee keeps the cash and pays the same fee online from a personal account.
- `cash_received` with an unpaid order is shown as **Cash received — online payment pending**.
- A manager can inspect cash-assisted onboarding records; there is no settlement or cash-deposit workflow.
- Cash declarations are immutable. There is no correction editor or settlement function in this release. Investigate mistakes through an authorized, documented corrective process that retains the original evidence; never directly change or delete a collection.
- The amount follows the registration's trusted fee snapshot. Employees see their own pending online-payment queue, including cash declarations, with instructions for resuming the original checkout. Collected cash is not company settlement liability in this product.
